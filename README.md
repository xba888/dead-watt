# Dead Watt

A pixel-art, top-down zombie defense game. Keep the generator running.

## Install on iPhone or iPad
1. Open the game's address in **Safari** (it has to be Safari on iOS).
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Launch it from the new Dead Watt icon. It opens fullscreen, and after the first launch it works offline.
4. Play in landscape. Flip the silent switch off if you want sound.

## Install on Android
Open the address in Chrome, open the menu, and choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

## Controls
- **Touch:** left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires. Swap, Reload and Sprint buttons sit on the right.
- **Keyboard + mouse:** WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to fire, Shift to sprint, R to reload, 1-4 to switch weapons.
- **Build phase:** pick Wall, Turret, Spikes or Mine, then tap or click the map.

## Files
- `index.html` – the whole game
- `manifest.webmanifest` – app name, icon and fullscreen settings
- `sw.js` – offline support
- `icons/` – app icons
